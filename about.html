About Solontrich
System

Built from real problems. Shipped for real people.

Who we are

Solontrich System is an independent software studio focused on building tools that actually get used. We don't build for the portfolio — we build because there's a gap, an inefficiency, a frustration that deserved a proper solution.

Every product in the collection started as a personal need. When no existing tool hit the mark, we built our own. That obsession with solving real problems is baked into everything we ship.

What we build

Our apps live at the intersection of freelance work, finance, and productivity. We focus on systems that handle the unsexy but critical parts of running an independent business — getting paid, staying organised, managing clients, and processing data without friction.

Right now the collection includes W.T.F. Freelancers, a peer-to-peer gig marketplace with direct payments and real-time messaging, and SanitizerAI Suite, a financial admin workspace covering data ingestion, batch payouts, ledger management, and professional invoice generation.

Our philosophy

Speed over bloat. Every feature earns its place. We'd rather ship something sharp and focused than pad out a product with things nobody asked for. If it doesn't solve the problem faster, cleaner, or more reliably — it doesn't ship.

We're also firm believers in keeping money in the hands of the people who earned it. That's why W.T.F. Freelancers takes zero platform cut and supports direct crypto and bank payments with no middleman involved.

The name

Solontrich is the system — the infrastructure, the suite, the studio behind the apps. It's a commitment to consistency: every product that carries the name is held to the same standard of quality, reliability, and thoughtful design.
