How we work

Solontrich System is a small, independent studio. We move fast, ship real products, and keep the team tight. There's no bureaucracy, no pointless meetings, and no artificial hierarchy. You own your work and your impact is visible immediately.

Everything is remote and async-first. We care about output, not hours logged.

What we value

  • Builders first. You ship. You don't just spec and hand off.
  • Ownership. You own your features from idea to deployment.
  • Honesty. We say what we think. Disagreement is healthy.
  • Craft. Details matter. We don't ship things we're not proud of.

No open roles right now

We're not actively hiring at the moment, but we're always interested in hearing from exceptional people. If you think you'd be a great fit for what we're building, reach out anyway — we read everything.
