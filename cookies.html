Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website or use a web application. They allow the service to remember information about your visit — such as your preferences or session state — so you don't have to re-enter it each time.

Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixel tags. This policy covers all of these.

2. Cookies we use

Essential cookies

These are required for our apps to function. They handle authentication sessions, security tokens, and user preferences. You cannot opt out of these without losing core functionality.

Performance cookies

These help us understand how users interact with our apps — which pages are visited most, where errors occur, and how load times can be improved. Data collected is aggregated and anonymous.

Functional cookies

These remember your settings and preferences, such as display mode or notification preferences, so you don't need to reconfigure them on every visit.

3. What we don't do

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not share cookie data with ad networks. We do not use cookies to build profiles for remarketing or third-party targeting purposes.

4. Third-party cookies

Some features of our apps may load content or scripts from third-party services — such as embedded fonts or analytics providers. These third parties may set their own cookies subject to their own privacy policies. We take care to partner only with providers who respect user privacy.

5. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse new cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Note that disabling essential cookies will affect the functionality of our apps.

For guidance on managing cookies in your specific browser, visit allaboutcookies.org.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our technology or legal obligations change. We'll update the date above and, where significant changes are made, notify you through the app or by email.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies or how we use them? Email us at solontrich@gmail.com.
