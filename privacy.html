Privacy
Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. What we collect

When you use Solontrich System apps, we may collect information you provide directly — such as account registration details (name, email address), profile information, and content you create or upload within the apps.

We also collect certain technical data automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and usage patterns within our applications. This helps us maintain performance, diagnose issues, and understand how our tools are used.

2. How we use your data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve our apps, provide customer support, send important service notifications, and detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes. Your data exists to make your experience with our tools work — nothing else.

3. Payments & financial data

Solontrich System facilitates direct peer-to-peer payments. We log transaction records (amounts, timestamps, parties involved) to provide order history and receipts. We do not store full payment credentials such as card numbers, bank account details, or private cryptographic keys. Payment processing is handled directly between parties or via trusted third-party processors.

4. Data sharing

We may share your information with third-party service providers that help us operate our platform — such as hosting providers, analytics services, and communication tools. All such providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and only for the purpose of delivering our services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Solontrich System or its users.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Certain records (such as transaction logs) may be retained for a period required by applicable law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure, but we take the protection of your data seriously and will notify you promptly of any breach affecting your information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you via email or in-app notice. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy? Reach us at solontrich@gmail.com.
